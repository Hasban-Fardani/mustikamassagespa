# Mustika visual asset notes

The editorial photography in the landing page is stored locally as compressed WebP files under `public/images/mustika/`. These images are atmospheric wellness references and are not presented as photographs of Mustika's actual premises or therapists.

Current production sources (downloaded and visually inspected on 17 August 2026):

- `hero-01-amber-spa.webp` and `hero-01-amber-spa-720.webp`: [Luxurious Spa Room with Warm Lighting](https://www.pexels.com/photo/luxurious-spa-room-with-warm-lighting-35495081/) by Burak Eroglu
- `hero-02-candle-massage.webp` and `hero-02-candle-massage-720.webp`: [A Couple Having a Massage](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-couple-having-a-massage-7365407/) by Sergey Torbik
- `hero-03-aroma-ritual.webp`, `hero-03-aroma-ritual-720.webp`, and `service-aromatherapy.webp`: [Scented Sticks and Candle](https://www.pexels.com/photo/scented-sticks-and-candle-7795827/) by Alesia Kozik
- `hero-04-traditional-care.webp`, `hero-04-traditional-care-720.webp`, and `hero-exit-traditional.webp`: [A Masseuse Massaging a Woman](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-masseuse-massaging-a-woman-6186763/) by Tima Miroshnichenko
- `service-traditional.webp`: [A Masseuse Doing a Massage](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-masseuse-doing-a-massage-6187254/) by Tima Miroshnichenko

The older `mustika-*.webp` files remain in the repository only as inactive archive assets and are no longer rendered by the landing page.

The source pages identify the images as free to use on Pexels. Review the source page and the applicable platform terms again before publishing any campaign or paid advertising that materially depends on a particular image.
